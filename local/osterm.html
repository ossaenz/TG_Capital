How this terminal works

A quick orientation — closes and won't show again on this browser.

Home

A glance view: overall market lean across every tracked ticker, your watchlist, and the most notable recent 8-K filings (material or bearish-flagged ones surface first).

Watchlist

Every ticker being tracked, each with a bullish/neutral/bearish status pulled from its latest Truth Check. Tap any ticker to drill into its detail.

Ticker Detail

Five real, independent signals, each in its own tap-to-expand card: SEC Sentiment (deterministic Loughran-McDonald word-count tone from the filing itself, no LLM), Public Consensus (crawled sentiment vs. that SEC tone), 8-K Intel (catalyst/bearish-signal analysis on real filings), Sentiment Tape (both scores over time), and Recent Hits (the raw filing history, exportable as CSV).

Search

Type any ticker and hit enter. If it isn't tracked yet, it's added and analyzed live right there — real SEC filing fetch, 8-K analysis, and a Truth Check run, shown step by step as each one actually finishes.

Every number on this page is either a real, already-computed signal or an honest "no data yet" state — nothing is invented to fill a layout. The few boxes still labeled "Coming Soon" (News/Social/Alt-data breakdown, Sector/Theme) are real gaps, not hidden data.
